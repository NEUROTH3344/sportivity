Privacy Policy

Sportivity GmbH takes your privacy seriously. Here is how we handle your data.

Note: this website is a mockup created for academic purposes.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and how we use it, and what rights you have regarding your personal data.

1. What We Collect

During your use of Sportivity, we may collect the following:

  • Information you provide when registering: name, email address, sport preferences etc.
  • Data submitted via contact forms, including your name, email, and message
  • Location data, if you choose to share it, to show nearby athletes
  • Usage data such as matched people, past sessions, and general interactions with the platform
  • Cookies to improve your experience and remember your preferences

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data to:

  • Connect you with nearby athletes
  • Respond to messages or questions submitted via the contact form
  • Improve and maintain the platform
  • Send notifications if you accept to receive them

3. Cookies

Cookies help us remember your preferences and improve your experience on our platform. They may be used for session management and basic analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features, such as staying logged in, may not work as expected. Your session cookies are deleted immediately after closing the browser and your persistent cookies are deleted after 6 months to comply with privacy directives.A renewal of consent will be requested at that time.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party tools such as analytics services to better understand the needs of our users. No personally identifiable data is sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Data Security

We take extensive technical and organizational measures to protect your data. You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you at any time.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The most current version will always be available on this page. Continued use of the platform after changes are posted, constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us at privacy@sportivity.com or via our contact page.
