What is Sportivity?

We built Sportivity because finding people to play sports with is surprisingly hard. People are becoming increasingly less active and it can be hard for someone to practice sport alone. Sportivity fixes that.

You set up a profile with the sports you play, your rough location and skill level, and we show you people nearby who are looking for the same thing. From there it's up to you — message them, agree on a time, and show up.

Pick your sports

Football, tennis, running, climbing — we cover over 30 sports. Filter by how seriously you play.

Find people nearby

See athletes in your city who are actually looking for partners or teammates right now with our innovative pair-finding algorithm SportMatch©️.

Meet up and play

Message directly, agree on a time and place, and go. No complicated scheduling tools needed.
