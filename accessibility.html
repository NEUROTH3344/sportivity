Accessibility

Sportivity is committed to making our website usable for everyone.

Our commitment

We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA conformity. This means we strive to ensure our website is understandable and usable for all users, including those with disabilities.

To support this, Sportivity has implemented the following measures:

  • Consistent navigation across all pages
  • Sufficient color contrast between site elements and background
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation and interactive elements
  • Alternative text for all images
  • Responsive layout that adapts to all screen sizes

This website is partially conformant with WCAG 2.2 Level AA. This means that some parts of the content may not fully meet all accessibility requirements, particularly embedded third-party content such as YouTube videos.

This statement was prepared on 9 May 2026. It is based on a self-assessment carried out by Sportivity GmbH.

Feedback

If you run into accessibility issues on our site or have suggestions for improvement, we would like to hear from you. You can reach us at accessibility@sportivity.com or through our contact page.

If you are not satisfied with our response to your concern, you may contact the relevant supervisory authority. In Austria, this is the Austrian Research Promotion Agency (FFG - Österreichische Forschungsförderungsgesellschaft) at ffg.at
